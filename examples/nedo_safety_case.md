# Example — NEDO Safety & Ethical Decision Case (Illustrative)

**Scenario:**  
A collaborative research project funded by NEDO identifies a potential safety issue in an energy storage prototype.  
The team must decide whether to **pause the project** for further risk assessment or **continue under monitored operation**.

---

## 🎯 Core Understanding (P0)
- **Goal:** Balance innovation progress with human and environmental safety.  
- **Constraints:** Funding timeline, partner accountability, technical readiness level (TRL 5).  
- **Success metric:** Zero critical incidents while maintaining project continuity.

---

## 🔍 Multi-Angle Analysis (P1)
| Axis | Evaluation |
|------|-------------|
| **Semantic** | The report indicates “abnormal temperature rise” in early-stage tests. |
| **Ethical** | Human safety > research schedule. Non-maleficence applies. |
| **Emotional** | Team stress, media sensitivity. |
| **Cultural** | In Japan, transparency with regulatory bodies is key. |
| **Causal** | Root cause likely material fatigue; mitigation path known. |

---

## 🧪 Verification (P2)
- Reviewed NEDO safety protocols (Rev.2024).  
- Confirmed preliminary data reproducibility.  
- Identified need to replicate environmental stress tests to validate robustness.  

---

## 🔄 Counter-Arguments (P3)
- **Option 1:** Continue testing under stricter thermal monitoring.  
  - **Counterpoint:** Residual risk not quantified; may breach NEDO guideline 4.3.  
- **Option 2:** Temporary halt and root-cause study.  
  - **Counterpoint:** May delay milestone payments.  
- **Option 3:** Escalate to NEDO Safety Board for joint review.  
  - **Counterpoint:** Adds administrative time but ensures institutional backing.  

---

## ✅ Recommendations (P4)
| Option | Summary | Expected Outcome | Risk | Mitigation |
|---------|----------|------------------|------|-------------|
| **A. Pause & Investigate** | Maintain safety compliance; use downtime for model validation. | Validated root cause | Delay | Use interim simulation tasks. |
| **B. Controlled Continuation** | Proceed under 24h monitoring + third-party audit. | Continuity with monitored data | Moderate | Add external ethics oversight. |

---

## 🔎 Completeness Check (P5)
- **Data adequacy:** Additional environmental and stress-cycle replication required before final go/no-go.  
- **Stakeholder review:** NEDO safety officer and ethics board to confirm compliance path.  
- **Readiness for decision:** Analysis sufficiently robust for provisional recommendation (Option A), pending external validation.  
- **Decision path:** Proceed to co-judgment after verification completion.

---

## 🔁 Co-Judgment (P6)
**Preferred:** Option A — *Safety-first, transparent communication with NEDO HQ.*  
**Rationale:** Aligns with ethical axis and regulatory expectations.  
**Next step:** Publish safety note + update partner report.
